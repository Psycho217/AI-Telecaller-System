// Capabilities

Everything a great SDR does —
without the ramp time.

Nevara runs outbound, qualifies, handles objections, and syncs the whole conversation into your CRM. No scripts to maintain, no headcount to train.

Human-sounding voice

Sub-second latency, natural turn-taking, and backchannels ("mm-hmm", "got it") — your prospects can't tell it's AI.

40+ languages

Run campaigns in English, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic and more — with accent-aware voices that localize the pitch.

Calendar + CRM sync

Books directly into Google or Outlook. Pushes clean notes, intent scores, and next steps to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive.

Objection handling

Trained on 2M+ sales conversations. Pivots smoothly on price, timing, and authority objections — and knows when to back off.

Live intent scoring

Every call gets a 0–100 intent score you can route on — hot leads get a human SDR instantly, cold leads get nurtured.

Compliant by default

TCPA & GDPR aware. Do-not-call honoring, consent capture, recording disclosures, and SOC 2 Type II audited.

// How it works

Live in a week, not a quarter.

Step 01

Upload your lead list

Drop a CSV, sync from your CRM, or plug in via API. We enrich each lead with fresh firmographics automatically.

Step 02

Describe the pitch in plain English

No scripts. Tell Nevara what you sell and who you sell to — it writes the opening, qualifiers, and objection playbook.

Step 03

Watch meetings hit your calendar

Campaigns run 24/7. You get daily summaries, recordings, and clean notes — booked meetings land directly in your calendar.

Intent Engine

Score every lead, route the hot ones.

Nevara listens for buying signals — budget mentions, timeline words, competitive displacement — and scores each call in real time. Hot leads can be warm-transferred to a human rep mid-call.

  • Real-time 0–100 intent scoring
  • Warm transfer to rep on signal
  • Custom signal rules per campaign
